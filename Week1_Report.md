# Week 1: Data Science Orientation & Problem Definition in Beauty & Wellness

**Intern:** Sakshi Suresh Wankhade
**Internship:** Data Science with Python - Henry Harvin

### Business Problem
How can a beauty D2C brand predict customer churn and recommend personalized skincare products?

### Why Important?
- India beauty market $20B+ growth
- 60% customers churn due to wrong product
- 71% want personalization (McKinsey)

### Research Questions
RQ1: What factors cause churn? (Recency, Rating, Frequency)
RQ2: Can we segment customers by skin type?
RQ3: Do similar skin users like same products?

### Data Needed
Customer Demographic, Transaction, Behaviour, Product Ingredients, Reviews

### Approach
Phase 1: EDA - RFM Analysis, WordCloud
Phase 2: Preprocessing - Handle missing skin type, sentiment score
Phase 3: Modeling - XGBoost for Churn, Collaborative Filtering for Recommendation, VADER for Sentiment
Phase 4: Dashboard & Marketing Action

Tools: Python, Pandas, Sklearn, NLTK, Surprise

### Conclusion
This will help reduce churn by 15-20% and increase CLV.
